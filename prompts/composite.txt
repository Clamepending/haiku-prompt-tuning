- Stock imagery: cherry blossoms, sakura, plum blossoms, morning fog, falling petals, a crow landing, a heron standing, "ancient pond" Bashō pastiche

- Sentiment abstractions as the emotional payload ("peace", "wonder", "melancholy", "contemplation")
